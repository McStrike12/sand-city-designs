How a software project works

We start by understanding how your business runs today.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn how your operations work, where time goes, and what the system needs to do.

  2. 02

    Scope

    We define the features, priorities and plan, so you know what's being built before we build it.

  3. 03

    Design

    We map the screens and workflows, and review them with you.

  4. 04

    Build & test

    We build in stages, so you can see progress and give feedback along the way.

  5. 05

    Launch & support

    We roll it out, help your team get started, and keep improving it.
