Material Distributors

Material Distributors, Inc. is a family-owned specialty building products distributor in Bala Cynwyd, PA, serving the construction industry since 1947. Sarah designed and built their entire website: a site that presents their product range, their landmark projects and their partners, and makes it easy for contractors to reach out.

Scroll inside the browser to explore the full site

The client

Material Distributors supplies Division 10, 11 and 12 specialty products to commercial, hospitality, residential and multipurpose construction projects. They work with more than 500 manufacturers worldwide and a team large enough to handle over 300 projects at once.

Their product range runs from toilet partitions, lockers and shower doors to visual display boards, wall protection, bike racks and fire equipment, and their projects include Philadelphia Union Stadium, Amazon's headquarters, The Wharf in Washington, DC and the Comcast tower in Philadelphia.

The challenge

With more than 70 years in business, a huge catalog and a portfolio of landmark buildings, Material Distributors needed a website that matched the scale of their work.

  • Show a wide product range without overwhelming visitors.
  • Prove their track record with real projects.
  • Explain what sets them apart: capacity, one contract for many scopes, and established vendors.
  • Give contractors a quick way to ask about an upcoming job.

The approach

Sarah designed the site around how contractors and specifiers look for a supplier: what can you provide, where have you done it, and how do I reach you.

A bold navy-and-white look with skyline photography puts their projects front and center, and a simple five-page structure (Home, About, Products, Projects and Contact) keeps every part of the business one click away.

The solution

  • A homepage that tells the whole story: the headline promise, the company background, "What sets us apart", previews of products and projects, trusted partners and a contact form.
  • A products page that organizes the catalog into clear categories, from toilet accessories to closet shelving and entrance mats.
  • A projects portfolio featuring their landmark work, from stadiums to corporate headquarters.
  • Trust signals throughout: manufacturer partners such as Moen, Saris and Foreman, plus SBA, OSHA and U.S. Green Building Council badges.
  • A built-in contact form so contractors can reach out about a project or question in seconds.

The outcome

Material Distributors now has a site that reflects more than 70 years of experience: a clear catalog, a portfolio of recognizable projects, and a direct line for new inquiries, all in one place.
