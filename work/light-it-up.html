Light It Up

Light It Up is a free one-day event from This Is It Team, held in Anaheim, California on October 18, 2026, for attendees in the room and online around the world. Mike designed and built the system behind it: the registration page, a personal attendee dashboard, live translation, and the tools the team uses to run the day.

Visit the event page

The client

This Is It Team runs events for a large international community. Light It Up was a free Sunday experience at the ACC North Building in Anaheim, held right after the Luminate conference, with a free virtual option for anyone who couldn't be there in person.

The challenge

One event, two audiences and many languages. The team needed more than a sign-up form.

  • Free registration for both in-person and virtual attendees.
  • One place for each attendee to find the live stream, replays, photos and event-day details.
  • Support for attendees who speak languages other than English, before and during the event.
  • Simple tools for the team to check people in, add walk-ins and keep everyone informed.

The approach

Instead of stitching together a ticketing site, a video host and an email tool, Mike built one connected system around the way This Is It Team runs its events. Every attendee gets one account tied to their email, and everything they need lives behind it.

The look matches the event's own artwork, so registration, the dashboard and the translation page all feel like part of Light It Up.

The solution

  • Registration page: the event artwork, date and venue, a live countdown, free RSVP for in-person attendees and a separate free virtual-access path, all switchable between English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French and Chinese.
  • Attendee dashboard: a personal portal where each attendee signs in with a password or an emailed sign-in link and finds a welcome page, the live stream, replays, event photos, a shop, in-person details with a venue map, and their account settings.
  • Live translation page: a no-login page, reachable by QR code in the room, where attendees put in their earbuds and pick their language to listen to live interpretation.
  • Team dashboard: a secure admin area with two-step login for managing events, QR tickets, a phone-camera check-in scanner that keeps working offline, walk-in registration, stream links for each language, past-event photos and announcement emails to in-person and virtual registrants.

The outcome

This Is It Team now runs its events on its own platform. Light It Up went from registration to live stream to replay in one place, and the same system also powers their paid Fearless event, with secure online checkout.
