Case study · Website Design & Branding

Personalized Performance

Personalized Performance is Luke's personal training business in Somers Point, New Jersey, offering in-person coaching and online programs. Sarah designed and built their website: a site that shows who Luke is, how he trains, and who he helps, and turns visitors into free consultations.

Visit the site

The client

Luke is a NASM Certified Personal Trainer and Performance Enhancement Specialist who trains clients in person at Great Bay Performance in Somers Point, NJ, and coaches online clients anywhere. He works with athletes, busy professionals and everyday people, and sells a six-week app-based workout program for advanced lifters.

The challenge

Personal training is a personal decision. Before anyone books a session, they want to know who their coach is, how he trains and whether he's right for them.

  • Introduce Luke and his credentials in a way that feels personal, not like a sales page.
  • Explain two very different offers: 1:1 coaching and a self-guided program.
  • Speak to three audiences at once: athletes, busy professionals and beginners.
  • Make the next step, a free consultation, obvious from anywhere on the site.

The approach

Sarah built the site like a conversation with a coach: who I am, how I train, how I can help, who it's for, what clients say, and how to start.

A dark, gritty gym look with bold gold branding and real training photography gives it energy, while warm cream sections keep the personal parts, like Luke's story, approachable.

The solution

  • A focused hero: "Personal Training That Fits Your Life", with the location and a clear call to book.
  • About Luke: his story, from high school lacrosse to coaching, plus his NASM and CPR/AED credentials.
  • Training philosophies: Train with Purpose, Keep It Fresh and Progress That Lasts.
  • Two offers side by side: 1:1 personalized coaching and the All Around Workout Program, each with what's included, and the program linking straight to purchase in its training app.
  • Who it's for: full-width photo sections for busy professionals, athletes and everyday fitness.
  • Proof and fit: what makes the coaching different, client testimonials and a "You'd be a great fit if…" checklist.
  • Calls to action throughout leading to a free consultation, plus FAQ and contact pages.

The outcome

Personalized Performance now has a site that feels like meeting Luke: personal, energetic and clear about how to get started, whether that's booking a consultation or buying a program.
