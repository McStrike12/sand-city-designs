Ever-Clear Pool Service

Ever-Clear Pool Service is a family-owned pool cleaning and maintenance company in Telford, Pennsylvania, caring for in-ground pools since 1986. Sarah designed and built their website: a site that feels as fresh as a clean pool and makes it easy for local homeowners to get in touch.

Visit the site

The client

Ever-Clear is a family-run pool company serving Telford, Bucks County and Montgomery County, including Hatfield, Souderton, Harleysville, Lansdale and Doylestown. They handle seasonal openings and closings, valet service, repairs, inspections, safety covers, pressure testing, renovations and automation.

The challenge

Homeowners choosing a pool company want to know three things fast: do you service my area, do you do what I need, and can I trust you with my pool.

  • Show up for local searches like "pool service in Telford, PA".
  • Present a long list of services clearly.
  • Carry the trust of a family business with nearly four decades of experience.
  • Give homeowners an easy way to call, email or send a request.

The approach

Sarah leaned into the feeling of a clean, ready-to-swim pool: crystal-blue water photography, a warm cream background, deep navy and a bright orange accent drawn from the sun in their logo. Wave-shaped section dividers tie the whole page together.

The headline names the service and the town right away, so both visitors and search engines know exactly what Ever-Clear does and where.

The solution

  • A local-first hero: "Pool Cleaning & Pool Service in Telford, PA", with the counties they serve right underneath.
  • Their story: family-owned since 1986, specializing in in-ground pools, with a clear Get in Touch button.
  • Pool and spa services at a glance: every service in one navy section, linking to a detailed services page, plus the towns they cover.
  • Customer reviews in a sliding carousel.
  • A closing call to action, "Where Clean Pools Meet Peace of Mind", leading to a request form.
  • A footer that works hard: hours, address, a tap-to-call phone number and email.
  • Supporting pages for services, the team, FAQs, a project gallery and contact.

The outcome

Ever-Clear now has a bright, trustworthy site that shows local homeowners exactly what they do and where, with a direct path to a call or service request from every page.
