This Is It Team

This Is It Team is a community of LifeWave brand partners. Sarah designed and built thisisitinfo.com, an information hub they share with people they introduce to the LifeWave X39 patch, so every conversation can point to one clear, polished place.

Visit the site

The client

This Is It Team is a network of LifeWave brand partners who introduce people to the X39 patch. Their members share the product in conversations, on calls and at events, and needed somewhere to send people afterwards.

The challenge

When someone hears about X39 for the first time, they have questions: what is it, how do you use it, who's behind it. The team needed one link that answers those questions clearly and consistently, no matter which member shared it.

  • Introduce the product in a clean, premium way.
  • Organize a lot of information, including instructions, explainer videos, patents and company background, without overwhelming visitors.
  • Send every visitor back to the person who referred them, and invite them onto the team's calls.

The approach

Sarah took her cues from the product's own packaging: deep blues, crisp white space and bold, modern type. The page moves from a big first impression to plain, practical information, in the order a curious visitor would ask their questions.

The solution

  • A bold hero: "Have You Been Patched?" beside the product, with Elevate, Activate and Regenerate as a quick visual tagline.
  • A product introduction with lifestyle photography showing how the patch is worn.
  • How to Wear X39: simple, icon-led instructions anyone can follow.
  • X39 Explained: a hub of explainer videos, plus sections for patents and studies, results and case reports.
  • Company background on LifeWave and its founder.
  • Built for sharing: a "Join our X39 calls" button in the header, a language switcher, and a closing note sending visitors back to the person who referred them.

The outcome

This Is It Team members now have one polished link to share. Every introduction leads to the same clear information, and back to the member who made it.
