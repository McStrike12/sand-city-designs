Beach City Baseball CRM Dashboard

Beach City Baseball Academy runs private lessons, cage rentals, clinics and camps with a full staff of coaches. Mike designed and built the staff portal they run it all from: scheduling, members, messaging, sales, payroll and reporting in one place.

Scroll inside the browser to see more of the app

The client

Beach City Baseball Academy is a training facility offering private lessons, batting cages, clinic series and camps for young players. A busy day means many instructors, multiple cages and back-to-back sessions, plus the families, packages and payments behind every booking.

The challenge

Running an academy like this usually means juggling a booking app, a payment system, spreadsheets for payroll and a separate inbox for families. The academy needed one system that fit the way they actually operate.

  • See every instructor, cage and lesson for the day at a glance.
  • Know who's new, whose package is running out and who still owes payment.
  • Keep members, players, prospects and conversations in one place.
  • Ring up sales, run programs, pay coaches and see how the business is doing, without switching tools.

The approach

Mike built the portal around the front desk's day. The schedule is the home screen, and every other tool sits one click away in a single navigation bar.

The design carries the academy's own branding, with its navy, red and wave logo, so the tool feels like theirs rather than generic software.

The solution

  • Schedule: a day view with a row for every instructor, showing each lesson by cage and time, with markers for new clients, the last session in a package, payment due and bookings hidden from the public. Staff can jump to any date or add a booking.
  • Members, prospects and coaches: families and their players, incoming prospects and the coaching staff, each with their own records.
  • Messages: conversations with families kept inside the portal.
  • Point of sale: find a member by name, email or phone and ring up a sale, with gift card balance lookups.
  • Programs: clinic series, camps and events with pricing, dates, age ranges, enrollment tracking and signup forms.
  • Payroll and partners: coach pay and partner relationships managed alongside the rest of the business.
  • Reports: a dashboard of revenue, sessions, average session value and players served, plus detailed reports for revenue, accounts receivable, coaches, purchase history, promo codes and gift cards.
  • Secure staff sign-in with role-based access for admins and staff.

The outcome

Beach City Baseball Academy runs its front desk, coaching schedule, sales and reporting from one system built around how they work, instead of a stack of separate apps.
